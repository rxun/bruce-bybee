Intentional Photography for New York's Cultural Landscape

Manhattan-based photographer specializing in performances, exhibitions, and portraiture.

Bruce Bybee is a Manhattan-based photographer working with artists, institutions, and cultural organizations. He creates atmospheric, precise imagery for live performances, exhibitions, public programs, and portrait sessions. With a background in film and over a decade of experience spanning analog and digital photography, he brings clarity, structure, and consistency to every assignment. Available for commissions in Manhattan, Brooklyn, and beyond.
